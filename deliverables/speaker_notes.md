# Advisor Pulse — Speaker Notes

**Timing guide (12-slide ideation deck):** ~8–10 minutes + Q&A.  
**Finale pitch:** compress to slides 1, 4, 7–8, 9/12 (official 2–4 slides).

---

## Slide 1 — Title
“Judges, our product is **Advisor Pulse**. The question every advisor must answer every day is: *Where does this client stand?* We are an investor-intelligence and attention-prioritization layer — not another CRM, not another chart wall. You’ll see a live demo book of 220 synthetic clients with a computed review queue.”

## Slide 2 — Problem
“Advisors already have data. What they lack is continuous, explainable attention across a book that often exceeds capacity. Cerulli’s 2026 AI study found most RIAs use AI to cut admin work and expand capacity — not to replace advice. Our hypothesis: the workflow gap is prioritization with evidence.”

## Slide 3 — Why existing tools fall short
“Be fair: Salesforce engages households; Morningstar researches; Aladdin monitors risk at institutional depth. We are not claiming they do nothing. We claim the **cross-context loop** — goal path vs actual, why the gap, why now, what to prepare — is still fragmented, especially for the long tail.”

## Slide 4 — Solution
“Advisor Pulse workflow: Detect → Explain → Prioritize → Prepare → Act → Learn. Differentiation is the combination: transparent factors, evidence-linked summaries, human-approved actions.”

## Slide 5 — How it works
“Analytics first, language second. Structured metrics feed prioritization; summaries only restate those facts. Security boundary: no sensitive data to external models without a DPA. Demo uses deterministic templates.”

## Slide 6 — 20 vs 200
“Greatest incremental value is the **200**, not replacing judgment on the **20**. Known clients get sharper meeting prep. Long-tail clients get proactive, thresholded signals. Numbers on this slide are illustrative unless labeled Cerulli.”

## Slide 7 — Investor summary
“Walk Priya Sharma — DEMO. Behind expected path, mid-cap drift, overdue advice. Everything on screen is computed from synthetic data.”

## Slide 8 — Live demo
“Tell the six-step story, then contrast Arjun Mehta: absolute AUM down because of a planned withdrawal — system does **not** escalate. We’re not a red-badge factory.”

## Slide 9 — Privacy
“SEBI makes the investment adviser responsible for AI tools and client data. DPDP informs consent and purpose limitation. Prototype is local demo — not certified.”

## Slide 10 — Competitive positioning
“Point to the matrix: we win on explainable attention workflow; we do **not** claim deeper risk engines than Aladdin.”

## Slide 11 — Roadmap
“What’s real today vs what needs custodian/CRM integrations. Pilot metrics are a plan — no fake results. Note Globathon Round 2 rebuild rule.”

## Slide 12 — Close
“Return to the question. Less time searching. More time acting on the right clients.”

## Suggested answers to hard interruptions
See `judge_qa.md` for the full set. Short versions:

- **Why not CRM?** CRM stores relationships; we decide *who needs attention why* with evidence.
- **Alert fatigue?** Configurable thresholds, confidence, data-quality separated from financial risk.
- **AI hallucinations?** Numbers from analytics only; LLM optional paraphrase disabled by default.

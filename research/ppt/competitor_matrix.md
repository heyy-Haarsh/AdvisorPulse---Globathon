## Positioning statement (fair)

Existing tools **store clients**, **analyze portfolios**, or **surface enterprise risk**. Advisor Pulse is an **investor-intelligence and action-prioritization layer** that connects goals, advice history, portfolio outcomes, and (optionally) cash-flow signals into a continuous **detect → explain → prioritize → prepare → act → learn** loop — with every alert showing *why*, *how confident*, and *what to review* — so advisors can allocate attention across the long tail without another chart-only dashboard.

## Mandatory challenge answers (deck narrative)

### 1. Differentiation vs CRM / portfolio trackers
CRM = relationship system of record. Portfolio trackers / research / Aladdin = analytics & risk depth. Advisor Pulse = **attention workflow** that answers *who needs review now, why, with what evidence, and what to prepare* — especially when context is fragmented across systems.

### 2. Value in the 20 vs the 200
- **Top ~20:** sharper meeting prep, changes-since-last-review, conversation-ready briefs (depth support).
- **Long-tail ~200:** greatest *incremental* value — proactive, thresholded detection so material issues surface without opening every portfolio.
- Demo book (~220 = 20 + 200) is **illustrative SYNTHETIC** storytelling aligned to Cerulli capacity research, not a measured productivity claim.

## What we will not claim

- That Salesforce/Aladdin/Morningstar “cannot” alert advisors.
- Invented market-share or pricing numbers.
- That our prototype is deeper risk analytics than Aladdin.
- Production compliance or regulatory approval.
